Welcome to AURA-OS v2.0 LTS
===========================

A real operating system environment running directly on top of the NitanshuOS Linux kernel.
All filesystem, process, network, and sandbox operations are real and verified.

- Terminal: Real /bin/bash login shell with PTY
- Task Manager: Real /proc metrics and cgroups v2 resource tracking
- Sandbox: OverlayFS isolation for safe execution
